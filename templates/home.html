About Us

A boutique stay in Budapest's vibrant heart

Iza-Szondi Homes is a charming panzió set in a beautifully restored 19th-century building on Szondi utca. We offer modern, light-filled loft apartments — each with its own kitchenette, dining area, and stylish furnishings — perfect for short stays, longer visits, or remote work retreats.

Situated in Budapest's lively 6th district, you're just minutes from the city's best ruin bars, restaurants, thermal baths, and cultural landmarks.
